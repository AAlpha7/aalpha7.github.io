Lumentread Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Developer: Lance Huang

Lumentread ("the game") does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal information.

1. What we don't collect

The game has no accounts, does not connect to the internet while you play, and contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party SDKs. We have no access to your name, contact details, location, device identifiers or gameplay history.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the game keeps the following on your device:

  • Settings, such as language, sound, vibration and graphics quality.
  • Progress, such as research levels, tanks, achievements, codex entries, best scores and an unfinished run.
  • Statistics used for adaptive difficulty, upgrade suggestions and boss learning, computed only on your device.

This data stays on your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the game removes it. Depending on your settings, iOS may include it in your device backups, which Apple handles.

3. Services provided by Apple

The game is distributed through the App Store. Downloads, purchases and updates are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us crash reports and aggregate statistics in a form that does not identify you.

4. Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes

If we add online features in the future (for example Game Center leaderboards or iCloud sync), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
