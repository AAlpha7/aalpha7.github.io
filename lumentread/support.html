Lumentread Support

For questions, bug reports or suggestions, email support@9329.me. If you can, include your device model, iOS version and the game version (shown on the App Store page).

FAQ

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts and does not go online.

What happens if I delete the game?

Deleting the game also deletes your progress. If your device backup included the game's data, restoring from that backup brings it back.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game plays offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No.
