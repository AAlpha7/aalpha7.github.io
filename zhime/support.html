支持

启用键盘

  1. 打开「设置」→「通用」→「键盘」→「键盘」→「添加新键盘…」，选「默契」。
  2. 在任何输入框里按住或点按地球键 🌐，切换到默契。
  3. （可选）想要按键震动：在「设置」→「通用」→「键盘」→「键盘」→「默契」里打开「允许完全访问」。只用于震动，不联网、不收集数据。

常见问题

需要联网吗？不需要。所有功能都在手机上运行，飞行模式下也一样。

怎么打出「嗯」？输入 en。

怎么清除学到的词？没在打字时点键盘左上角的齿轮进入设置，点「清除记住的短语」。删除 App 会把学到的和统计的全部删除。

能简拼吗？能。按词打首字母，例如 wmyqqcf → 我们一起去吃饭。

为什么没有震动？iOS 规定第三方键盘要开「完全访问」才能震动；另外请确认键盘设置里「按键震动」是开着的。

Support

Turn on the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard…, and choose 默契 (Tacit).
  2. In any text field, tap or hold the globe key 🌐 to switch to it.
  3. (Optional) For haptic feedback on key presses, turn on Allow Full Access under Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → 默契. It is used only for haptics; no network, no data collected.

FAQ

Does it need the internet? No. Everything runs on the iPhone, in Airplane Mode too.

How do I clear what it learned? When not typing, tap the gear at the keyboard's top left and choose 清除记住的短语 (Clear remembered phrases). Deleting the app deletes everything it learned and counted.

Can I type abbreviations? Yes: the first letter of each syllable, e.g. wmyqqcf → 我们一起去吃饭.

Why no haptics? iOS lets a third-party keyboard use haptics only with Full Access; also check that 按键震动 (key haptics) is on in the keyboard's settings.
