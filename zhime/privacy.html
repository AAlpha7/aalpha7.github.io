隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日

一句话

默契不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。App 和键盘都没有任何网络请求。

你打的字

拼音转换、候选排序（包括本地 AI 小模型）、联想和表情都在你的 iPhone 上完成。你输入的文字不会离开手机，我们看不到，也没有任何第三方能看到。

键盘学到的词

为了越用越顺手，键盘会记住你选过的词和短语，以及「你的输入」统计卡上的数字（打了多少字、首选命中率等）。这些只存放在键盘自己在你手机上的存储空间里，不同步、不上传。记住的短语可以在键盘设置里随时清除（「清除记住的短语」）；删除 App 会把键盘学到的和统计的全部删除。

完全访问

键盘申请「完全访问」只为两件事：按键震动，以及读取你在 App 里选的键盘主题。iOS 规定第三方键盘要开完全访问才能使用震动反馈，也才能读到 App 和键盘在你手机上共用的设置；主题选择只存在你的手机上。不开完全访问，所有打字功能照常可用；开了也不会联网，不会读取或发送任何数据。

不使用的东西

  • 没有账号，不需要登录。
  • 没有广告，没有统计或分析 SDK，没有追踪。
  • 不使用麦克风、相机、位置、通讯录。
  • 不读取剪贴板。

儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果以后加入需要联网或收集数据的功能，我们会先更新本页，并在 App 里说明，由你选择是否使用。

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

In short

默契 (Tacit) collects, uploads and shares no data at all. Neither the app nor the keyboard makes any network request.

What you type

Pinyin conversion, candidate ranking (including the small on-device AI model), next-word suggestions and emoji all run on your iPhone. What you type never leaves the device; we cannot see it and neither can any third party.

Words the keyboard learns

To get better as you use it, the keyboard remembers the words and phrases you pick and the numbers on its "Your typing" card (characters typed, first-choice rate and so on). They are stored only in the keyboard's own storage on your iPhone, never synced or uploaded. Remembered phrases can be cleared at any time in the keyboard's settings (清除记住的短语, Clear remembered phrases); deleting the app deletes everything the keyboard learned and counted.

Full Access

The keyboard asks for Full Access for two things only: haptic feedback on key presses, and reading the keyboard theme you pick in the app. iOS requires Full Access for a third-party keyboard to use haptics and to read settings the app and keyboard share on your phone; the theme choice stays on your phone. Without Full Access every typing feature works; with it, the keyboard still makes no network connection and reads or sends no data.

What we don't use

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no tracking.
  • No microphone, camera, location or contacts.
  • No clipboard reading.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future feature ever needs the network or any data, we will update this page first and explain it in the app, and you decide whether to use it.
